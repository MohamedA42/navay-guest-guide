HOUSE RULES

Help us look after the home

01

Quiet hours

Quiet hours are from 11:00 pm to 7:00 am. Please keep noise, including TV volume, to a minimum out of respect for our neighbours.

02

No smoking or vaping

Smoking, vaping and the use of illegal substances on or around the property are strictly prohibited. Charges of up to £500 may apply.

03

No parties

Parties are strictly prohibited. Any violation may incur extra charges and guests may be asked to leave.

04

Registered guests only

Only guests registered on the booking are permitted to stay at the property.

05

No pets

Pets are not permitted at the property.

06

Keys & lockouts

Please take extra care of your keys. Lost keys incur a minimum £100 charge, and a £50 call-out fee applies if you are locked out.

07

Breakages

If anything breaks or is damaged during your stay, please let us know as soon as possible so it can be replaced promptly.
